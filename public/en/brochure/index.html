Free, multilingual guidance for families after the loss of a loved one.

Clarvia is a Luxembourg non-profit building free, multilingual, source-backed bereavement guidance - starting in Luxembourg, designed for cross-border European realities.

For families, communes, social workers, professionals, volunteers, and public-interest funders.

Why Clarvia Exists

The Problem

Families are grieving, yet must make urgent administrative decisions. The information exists - but it is scattered, technical, and hard to act on at the worst possible moment.

What Clarvia Does

Two Free Tools

Clarvia is developing two complementary tools - both free, both designed with dignity and privacy in mind.

Bereavement Checklist
Currently available in English, French, and German.
  • What to do first
  • Which documents to collect
  • Who to notify
  • When to seek professional advice
Heritage Folder
An optional, privacy-conscious space to preserve wishes, memories, documents, contacts, and practical information.
Clarvia provides administrative guidance based on official sources. It does not replace legal, tax, or medical advice.

Source-Backed & Verifiable

How It Works

Clarvia Graph is the open knowledge engine behind the checklist.

Every checklist item follows a verifiable chain:

1
Official source
2
Archived snapshot
3
Extracted assertion
4
Structured rule
5
Checklist item

This means guidance can be validated, corrected, and reused by any public-interest service.

Privacy by Design
Checklist rules run locally in the browser, so sensitive family answers are never sent to Clarvia's servers.
